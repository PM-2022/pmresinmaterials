Store policy

Please read our store policy carefully before placing an order. By placing an order on our website, you agree to the following terms and conditions.

1. Product Availability

All products are subject to stock availability. Although we try to keep our website stock updated, occasional stock discrepancies may occur.

If an item is unavailable after you place an order, we will inform you and issue a refund for that item. The remaining available items will be dispatched.

2. Order Processing & Dispatch

  • Orders confirmed before 10:00 AM are usually dispatched on the same business day.
  • Orders confirmed after 10:00 AM will usually be dispatched on the next business day.
  • Our working days are Monday to Saturday, excluding national holidays.
  • Dispatch times may vary due to holidays, high order volumes, or other unexpected circumstances.

3. Shipping & Delivery

Estimated delivery times after dispatch:

  • Tamil Nadu & Puducherry: 1–3 business days.
  • Other states: 3–7 business days.

These are estimated delivery times and may vary depending on the courier service, location, weather, or other unforeseen circumstances.

Shipping charges are calculated at checkout based on the order's weight, dimensions, and delivery location.

4. Change of Delivery Address

If you need to change your shipping address, please contact us as soon as possible through WhatsApp at 7695976223.

Address changes can only be made before the order is dispatched. Once the parcel has been dispatched, we cannot guarantee that the address can be changed.

5. Unboxing Video – Mandatory for Claims

A continuous, uncut 360° unboxing video is mandatory for any claim related to missing items, incorrect items, or transit damage.

To make a valid claim:

  • Start recording before opening the parcel.
  • Show all sides of the unopened package clearly.
  • Record the entire unboxing process without pauses, cuts, or editing.
  • Clearly show the items received and any missing, incorrect, or damaged products.
  • Submit your claim and video within 24 hours of receiving the parcel.

Claims without a clear, continuous unboxing video or claims submitted after the specified period may not be accepted.

6. Missing or Incorrect Items

If any item is missing or an incorrect item is received, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery and share the complete unboxing video.

After verification, we will provide a refund for the eligible item. We will not reship missing or incorrect items. If you still need the product, you may place a new order.

7. Parcel Damaged During Transit

We pack our products carefully, but damage may occasionally occur during transportation.

If your parcel or its contents arrive damaged:

  • Record the damage clearly in your continuous unboxing video.
  • Contact us within 24 hours of receiving the parcel.
  • Share the video and relevant photos with our customer support team.

After reviewing the evidence, we will process a refund for eligible damaged items. Refunds will not be provided if the damage cannot be verified from the submitted evidence.

Please note that we cannot guarantee that every parcel will arrive without transit damage.

8. Returns, Exchanges & Refunds

  • We do not accept returns or exchanges for change of mind, incorrect product selection, or personal preference.
  • Refunds may be provided for verified missing items, incorrect items, eligible transit damage, or items cancelled due to stock unavailability.
  • A continuous, uncut 360° unboxing video is mandatory for claims related to missing, incorrect, or damaged items. Claims must be reported within 24 hours of delivery.
  • Once a refund is approved, it will be processed within 2 business days. The time taken for the amount to reflect in your account may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.
  • Missing or incorrect items will be refunded if the claim is verified. We will not reship these items; you may place a new order if required.

Please check the product details, sizes, quantities, and descriptions carefully before placing your order.

9. Machinery & Lights – No Warranty

No warranty is provided on machinery or lights sold by PM Resin Materials.

Please check the product details carefully before placing your order. Any issue noticed upon delivery must be reported within 24 hours, along with a continuous, uncut 360° unboxing video.

Reporting an issue does not guarantee a refund. Any claim will be reviewed based on the evidence provided and applicable consumer rights.

10. Returned or Undelivered Parcels

If a parcel is returned to us due to an incorrect or incomplete address, the recipient being unavailable or unreachable, refusal to accept the parcel, or other reasons attributable to the customer, the customer must pay the applicable shipping charges for reshipping.

Please ensure that your name, phone number, and complete shipping address are correct before placing your order.

11. Tracking & Delivery Delays

For tracking details, please contact us on WhatsApp at 7695976223.

If your parcel has not arrived within the estimated delivery period, contact us so we can check with the courier service and assist you.

12. Customer Support

For order-related questions, shipping concerns, or claims, please contact us:

WhatsApp: +91 7695976223

Please provide your order number when contacting us so we can assist you faster.
